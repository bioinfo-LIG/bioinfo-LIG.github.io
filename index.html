Welcome!

The group Bioinfo belongs to the APTIKAL team, at the Laboratoire d'informatique de Grenoble: (LIG). Our research is dedicated to the development of methods and tools to analyse, interpret, and decipher biological data and processes, with a focus on cancer biology and microbiology.

We are composed of experts in biostatistics, bioinformatics and microbioloby.

Research interest

  • Biological questions

    • Organization of genomes
    • Origins and evolution of functions
    • Regulation of gene expression

  • Methodological development

    • Biophysics of DNA
    • Comparative genomics
    • Machine learning in high dimensions
