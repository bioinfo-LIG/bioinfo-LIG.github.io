Welcome!

The group Bioinfo belongs to the APTIKAL team, at the Laboratoire d'informatique de Grenoble: (LIG). Our bioinformatics research group is focused on the analysis and modeling of large-scale molecular data. Our methodological approach combines biostatistics, mathematical modeling, and machine learning to address complex biological questions, with a focus on cancer biology and microbiology.

We hold joint group meetings with Clovis Galiez's group, which focuses on computational metagenomics.

We belong to the BiGre network (Computational Biology in Grenoble) that gathers bioinformatics groups from different research units in Grenoble.

Research interest

  • Biological questions

    • Micro-evolution
    • Molecular and cellular variations
    • Tumor heterogeneity
    • Metagenomics

  • Methodological development

    • Bioinformatics and computational biology
    • Biostatistics and Machine learning
    • Genomics, epigenomics, transcriptomics
    • Multiomics data integration
