**[Antoine Frénoy](https://perso.crans.org/frenoy)** is an assistant professor in evolutionary biology and computer science. His main research interests include computational models for evolutionary dynamics, ecology and evolution in microbial communities, fitness landscapes and effects of mutations. He was part of the TIMC lab between 2019 and 2025, and joined the LIG (team Aptikal) in january 2026.
